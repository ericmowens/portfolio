## The content is used for the description of the project
---

While at FAST Enterprises, I worked on their project with the Vermont Department of Labor to redesign their Unemployment Insurance program. The overhaul included making a more accessible frontend site for claimants (which can be explored below) as well as improved system architecture on the back-end using FAST's core UI product to adhere to regulations. A lot of the work is hidden on the backend, but exploring the site at a surface level can help to give a clearer picture about what was changed with the project and how easy the system is to navigate now. I am proud of my work on this project, which is now live and being used by claimants across the state.
