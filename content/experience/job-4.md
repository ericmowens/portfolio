I currently work as a Software Escalation Engineer at Varonis. In this role, I act as technical point of contact for customer-impacting issues. These issues are presented in escalations, which are monitored tickets. Our escalations are monitored in order to make sure that customers' issues are resolved in a timely manner. I investigate and resolve issues with Varonis' data classification product across SQL databases, services, messaging, and UI. Additionally, in certain cases, I need to investigate other aspects of the software, including hardware and network issues. Most commonly, I will use Visual Studio to debug .dmp files of the service and analyze code that was running on the machine at the time of capture. Live debugging has also been a fascinating troubleshooting experience in this role. The variance in escalation issues and investigations is one interesting aspect of this role for me. I also act as an internal point of contact for my area of the product in case any support engineers have specific questions about functionality. Since joining my small product team, I have reduced the team’s average time-to-resolution for support tickets by 30%.

As part of an escalation case, I perform root cause analysis in collaboration with engineering and infrastructure teams. In many cases, an issue will cover the scope of several engineering teams, and communication is critical to ensure an efficient and effective resolution. After a support case is closed, we may also create tracking items to further improve the product and the customer experience. When necessary, I have created, tested, and deployed patches to live customer environments.

Since joining Varonis, I have gained the opportity to leverage GitHub Copilot and AI-powered workflows during the development and debugging process for our software. In the field of software, the landscape is ever-changing and requires constant research and learning as it evolves. These tools are used to streamline development, reduce the time-to-resolution of support tickets, and assist in documenting meetings, notes, and messages for support cases.
